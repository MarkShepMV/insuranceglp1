Does Blue Cross Blue Shield Cover Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, and Zepbound?

Blue Cross Blue Shield (BCBS) is not a single insurer — it is a federation of 34 independent regional companies, which makes answering coverage questions more complex than for a unified insurer like Aetna. Ozempic and Mounjaro for diabetes are broadly covered across BCBS affiliates. Coverage for Wegovy and Zepbound for weight loss varies substantially: the Federal Employee Program and some commercial group plans cover them, while other affiliates and plan types have excluded them as of 2026. This page summarizes what BCBS affiliates and the FEP formulary publicly disclose as of May 2026.

Important: BCBS coverage is affiliate-specific. BCBS of Texas, BCBS of Massachusetts, Anthem BCBS, and the Federal Employee Program each maintain separate formularies. This page summarizes published policies across major affiliates — always verify with your specific BCBS company.

Zepbound coverage under BCBS is the most restricted of the four GLP-1 medications. The BCBS FEP Blue Basic plan does not cover Zepbound; members are required to try both Wegovy and Saxenda before Zepbound can be considered, per FEP member accounts and FEP formulary documentation. The FEP program uses CVS Caremark, which has a Novo Nordisk partnership — this may structurally favor Wegovy over Zepbound. BCBS of Massachusetts explicitly excluded Zepbound from coverage for weight loss starting 2026, along with Saxenda and Wegovy for obesity indications. Anthem BCBS in Connecticut lists Zepbound at Tier 2 on the 2026 Essential Drug List with prior authorization and benefit design requirements, per Anthem CT's published formulary — one of the more favorable BCBS placements. BCBS of Texas coverage is largely employer-dependent. Nevada BCBS does not cover weight-loss GLP-1s on large group fully insured plans. Members with obstructive sleep apnea documentation may be able to obtain Zepbound coverage where it is otherwise excluded, as OSA represents a separate FDA-approved indication. Eli Lilly's Lilly Savings Card does not apply when insurance is billed — it is available for self-pay patients.

How to Check Your Specific BCBS Plan

Because BCBS is a federation of independent companies, your first step is identifying which BCBS affiliate administers your plan — it is printed on your member ID card. Then confirm coverage directly with that company.

  1. Look at your insurance card to identify your specific BCBS affiliate (e.g., Anthem, BCBS of Illinois, Blue Shield of California, FEP Blue).
  2. Call the member services number on the back of your card and ask to speak with Pharmacy Benefits.
  3. Ask specifically: "Is [medication name] covered on my formulary for [diabetes / weight management]?"
  4. Reference the NDC — Ozempic NDC: 0169-4060; Wegovy NDC: 0169-4550; Mounjaro NDC: 0002-1436; Zepbound NDC: 0002-1453.
  5. Ask: "Is prior authorization required, and what documentation must my doctor submit?"
  6. Ask: "Is my plan self-funded or fully insured?" — this affects whether state mandates apply.

Frequently Asked Questions — BCBS GLP-1 Coverage

Why does BCBS coverage vary so much for GLP-1 medications?

Blue Cross Blue Shield is a federation of 34 independently operated companies — BCBS of Texas, BCBS of Illinois, Anthem BCBS, BCBS of Massachusetts, and the Federal Employee Program each operate as separate insurers with separate formularies, clinical policies, and employer contracts. A policy decision made by BCBS of Massachusetts does not apply to Anthem BCBS in Connecticut. Self-funded employer plans, regardless of which BCBS affiliate administers them, can further customize their own formulary and may include or exclude weight-loss GLP-1s independently of the affiliated insurer's standard formulary.

Does BCBS FEP cover Zepbound?

The BCBS Federal Employee Program (FEP) Blue Basic plan does not cover Zepbound as of 2026. FEP Blue Basic uses CVS Caremark, which has a partnership with Novo Nordisk — this arrangement structurally favors Wegovy (semaglutide) over Zepbound (tirzepatide). For FEP Basic members who cannot tolerate Wegovy or Saxenda and have documentation such as obstructive sleep apnea, a formulary exception may be possible through the FEP prior approval process. FEP Blue Standard members should check their specific formulary, as coverage rules may differ.

Did BCBS of Massachusetts stop covering Wegovy and Zepbound?

Yes, with important caveats. BCBS of Massachusetts announced that starting in 2026, it would only authorize GLP-1 medications for Type 2 diabetes — it will no longer cover GLP-1s indicated primarily for obesity, including Wegovy and Zepbound, even for other FDA-approved conditions such as cardiovascular risk reduction and sleep apnea, for members using the Standard Formulary and Advanced Control Specialty Formulary. However, Harvard-sponsored BCBS and HUGHP plans administered through Express Scripts are not affected by this change and continue covering GLP-1s for weight loss through the ESI EncircleRx program.

What step therapy does BCBS typically require for GLP-1 weight-loss medications?

Step therapy requirements vary by BCBS affiliate. Anthem BCBS multi-state plans commonly require a prior trial of Contrave or Saxenda before approving Wegovy or Zepbound. BCBS of Illinois requires a documented six-month lifestyle intervention and step therapy with Contrave before GLP-1 approval. The FEP Blue Basic plan requires documented failure of both Wegovy and Saxenda before Zepbound is considered. All BCBS affiliates require prior authorization for weight-loss GLP-1s, and most require documentation of BMI criteria (typically 30 or greater, or 27 or greater with a qualifying comorbidity) and evidence of prior lifestyle modifications.

Are there any BCBS plans that offer strong GLP-1 weight-loss coverage in 2026?

The BCBS Federal Employee Program (FEP) Blue Standard offers the most accessible GLP-1 weight-loss coverage within the BCBS system — Wegovy is covered at specialty rates with prior authorization, and the FEP Standard specialty copay is $100 for a 30-day supply or 30% coinsurance. Anthem BCBS's Connecticut Essential Drug List places Zepbound at Tier 2 with prior authorization, making it one of the more favorable Zepbound placements in the BCBS federation. Self-funded employer plans administered by any BCBS affiliate can also opt to cover Wegovy and Zepbound — whether your employer made this election is the most important factor to determine first.
